How to format your quiz (MCQ & mixed question types supported)

Supported question types:
[mcq]   Multiple-choice
    [short] Short free-text answer
    [calc]  Calculation (numeric or symbolic)
    [code]  Code-reading question (single-line code block via ```...```)
Canonical single-line examples:
1. [mcq] What is 2+2? a) 3 b) 4 c) 5 d) 6 [ans: b]
    2. [short] Define overfitting. [ans: fitting noise in training data]
    3. [calc] Compute $3^2 + 1$. [ans: 10]
Important notes:
- One question per line
    - Question type tag is mandatory: [mcq], [short], or [calc]
    - [ans: ...] is mandatory and must appear on the same line
    - LaTeX must use single backslashes (e.g. \in, \mathbb{R})
    - Line breaks separate questions only
Short & calculation questions
Short & calculation questions are self-graded.
- After submitting your answer the correct answer will be shown
- You decide if your answer is correct and click Yes or No
- The system updates your score and advances to the next question
